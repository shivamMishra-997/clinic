About the Clinic

Care that starts with listening.

Clearview Clinic was built around a simple idea: most visits don't need to feel rushed or clinical. We take time to understand what's actually going on before reaching for a prescription pad.

Whether it's a routine check-up, a nagging cough, or ongoing management of blood pressure or diabetes, you'll see the same doctor visit after visit — no hand-offs, no repeating your history.

Plain-language explanations — no jargon, no rush.

Continuity of care with one physician, not a rotating roster.

Basic diagnostics and referrals handled the same day where possible.

Transparent, upfront consultation fees.

General Health Check-up

Vaccinations & Immunization

Preventive Health Screening

Health Certificates & Referrals

Fitness certificates, sick notes, and referrals to specialists when a concern needs one.
